# Settings View Surface Brief

- **Target:** `src/ui/screens/settings-view/`
- **Mode:** Operate
- **Audience & Scene:** an RVT student on a phone, one hand, usually in the corridor with the app opened for a specific reason: switch subgroup or building, find out why alerts are silent, pick a theme once. Teachers see only their own parameters.

## Direction contract

THESIS: order Settings by why a student opens it, not by the code's modules; surface what is broken, fold what is rarely touched.

OWN-WORLD: the incumbent Night/Day system (SettingsGroup rows, 54x32 lamp Switch, ThemePicker thumbnails, DS Chip). No new visual language; the change is structural.

STORY: identity and its two filters at the top, one status line (calm unless alerts are blocked), then Notifications, Appearance, Sharing, About, a folded "More settings", and the reset alone at the bottom.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the identity card with subgroup and building chips, the status line, and the start of Notifications; no sheet is needed for any choice of two or three.

STATES: no class picked; teacher (no subgroups); single building (no building chips); alerts blocked (caution line, button on native); offline (muted); desktop and iPhone-in-browser notification variants; Subjects and Transit tabs present or absent.
